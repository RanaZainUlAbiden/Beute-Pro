      Privacy Policy

      Who we are

      Our website address is beutepro.com. Béute Pro is a skincare brand based in Pakistan; products are formulated and filled on our own floor in Faisalabad.

      Information we collect

      When you use the contact form on this site, we collect the name, email address and message you submit, so we can reply to you. If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address for that purpose only.

      Cookies & local storage

      This site doesn't use tracking cookies. Your language preference, background-music setting and shopping cart contents are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only, so they're remembered on your next visit. Clearing your browser data clears them too.

      Third-party services

      Page fonts are served by Google Fonts. Loading them may send your device's IP address and browser information to Google, under Google's own privacy policy. [CLIENT TO CONFIRM] whether analytics, ad pixels or other third-party trackers are added later.

      How we use your information

      We use the information you send us to respond to your enquiry or fulfil your order, and nothing else. We never pass your email address on to anyone else.

      Your rights

      You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at any time — except where we're required to keep it for legitimate administrative, legal or security reasons.

      Contact

      Questions about this policy can be sent to support@beutepro.com.

      Refund & Exchange Policy

      Our refund and returns policy lasts 30 days from the date of purchase. If more than 30 days have passed, we can no longer offer a full refund or exchange.

      Eligibility

      • The item must be unused and in the same condition you received it, in its original packaging.
      • A receipt or proof of purchase is required.
      • For hygiene reasons, an opened or used soap, oil or mist can only be returned if it arrived defective or damaged.

      Sale items

      Only regular-priced items can be refunded. Sale items are final sale.

      Refunds

      Once your return is received and inspected, we'll email you to confirm whether it's approved. Approved refunds are credited to your original payment method [CLIENT TO CONFIRM — number of business days]. Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless the item arrived defective or damaged, in which case we cover it.

      Exchanges

      We only replace items that arrive defective or damaged. Contact us at support@beutepro.com before sending anything back.

      Gifts

      If the item was marked as a gift when purchased and shipped directly to you, you'll receive store credit for the value of the return. If it wasn't marked as a gift, the refund goes to the original purchaser.

      Questions

      Email support@beutepro.com any time.
